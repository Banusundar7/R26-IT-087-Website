R26-IT-087

An AI-Based Intelligent
Pomegranate Farming
System for Yield and Quality

Pomegranate farmers in Sri Lanka often make decisions without clear information. Our system uses IoT sensors and AI to help them check soil, detect diseases, predict harvest time and grade fruit, all in one mobile app.

Explore our research ↗ View documents →

Research Domain

Literature Survey

Past studies used CNNs to detect pomegranate diseases and grade fruit, transfer learning to classify growth stages, and IoT sensors to monitor soil and weather.

Each study solves only one farming task.

Research Gap

  • No single system covers soil, growth, disease and quality
  • Growth-stage studies do not predict harvest time
  • Grading studies do not suggest uses for low-quality fruit
  • IoT studies monitor only – no fertilizer or irrigation advice

Research Problem

Sri Lankan pomegranate farmers guess soil needs, notice diseases late, judge ripeness by eye and grade fruit by hand.

This leads to wasted resources, crop loss and low-value harvests.

Research Objectives

Main: one AI and IoT mobile app that supports farmers from soil to harvest.

  • Recommend fertilizer and irrigation from soil and weather data
  • Detect growth stage and predict harvest time
  • Detect diseases, measure severity and suggest treatment
  • Grade fruit quality and suggest uses for waste fruit

Methodology

  1. 1
    Data Collection – field photos, Kaggle & Mendeley datasets, ESP32 soil sensor and Open-Meteo weather
  2. 2
    Pre-processing – clean, resize to 224×224, augment and annotate images
  3. 3
    Model Training – Random Forest, MobileNetV2 CNN and YOLO, compared with SVM, KNN and ANN
  4. 4
    Integration – TensorFlow Lite models in a Flutter app; sensor data over Bluetooth
  5. 5
    Evaluation – accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and mAP on unseen test data

Core Components

01

Growth Stage Detection & Harvest Prediction

V.Harini

Growth Stage Detection & Harvest Prediction

The farmer takes a photo of the fruit, and the system identifies its growth stage: bud, flower, early fruit, mid-growth or mature. It then estimates the next stage and when the fruit will be ready to harvest, with care tips for each stage.

02

Disease Detection, Severity & Treatment

S.Banuja

Disease Detection, Severity & Treatment

From a fruit photo, the system detects Bacterial Blight, Anthracnose, Cercospora or Alternaria. It measures how much of the fruit is affected (mild, moderate or severe) and suggests a suitable treatment.

03

Fruit Quality Grading & Waste Utilization

A.Sowmiya

Fruit Quality Grading & Waste Utilization

Harvested fruits are graded as High, Medium or Low quality from a photo. For low-quality fruit, the system finds the defect (crack, rot, disease or sunburn) and suggests the best use: sale, juice processing, by-products or composting, reducing waste.

04

Fertilizer Recommendation & Irrigation Prediction

T.Kobiram

Fertilizer Recommendation & Irrigation Prediction

A portable IoT soil sensor reads nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, moisture and temperature from the field. Together with the tree's age and local weather, the app tells the farmer how much fertilizer is needed and whether the orchard needs watering.
